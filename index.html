About

I am a researcher and developer from Bangladesh working on AI for healthcare. My focus is safety-oriented health information tools: analysing medical reports, giving symptom context and helping patients prepare for clinician visits.

Alongside research I build full-stack web apps, compete in coding and AI contests, and study cybersecurity and networking.
